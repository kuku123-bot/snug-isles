Terms

Last updated: October 2026

Snug Isles is a free game made by one person (the "developer"). By playing it you agree to these simple terms. If something here does not make sense, ask on the project page before you pay for anything.

Playing

  • The whole game is free, and it will stay playable without paying anything. The Supporter Shop is optional.
  • The game is provided "as is", without promises. It may have bugs, and your saved islands live in your browser, so keep a backup file of worlds you care about.
  • Be kind to the people you play with. Only invite people you know, and you can say no to anyone who asks to join.

The Supporter Shop

  • The shop sells cosmetic extras only (a badge, a golden name, a rainbow trail, a crown). They change how you look and nothing else. They never make the game easier.
  • You pay in USDC on the Solana network from your own crypto wallet (Phantom, MetaMask or another Solana wallet), straight to the developer's wallet. The payment screen shows the exact amount and the receiving address before you approve anything in your wallet: 3uPkK4Xa6554PHtiPiob2EbGo6PX1nk3oA3r93nAQkni. If the address in your wallet is different, do not approve it.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars (USDC is meant to be worth one dollar). You also pay the tiny network fee in SOL, which goes to the Solana network, not to the developer.
  • Blockchain payments cannot be reversed. Because of that, and because the items are instant digital goods, all sales are final and there are no refunds, except where the law where you live says otherwise.
  • Your purchases belong to your wallet, not to an account (there are none). On a new device, connect the same wallet and press Restore my purchases. If you lose access to the wallet, the developer cannot move a purchase to another wallet.
  • If a payment went through but the item did not appear, wait a minute and press Restore my purchases. If it still does not show, send the transaction signature (from your wallet's history) on the project page and it will be sorted out.
  • The developer may change prices, add or remove items, or close the shop. Items you already bought are cosmetic and may stop working if the game one day stops being updated.

Your responsibilities

  • If you are young, ask a parent or guardian before buying anything.
  • Scams exist: nobody from Snug Isles will ever message you asking for money, a seed phrase or a wallet connection. Never share your seed phrase. The game never asks for it.
  • USDC is issued by Circle and the Solana network and your wallet are run by other people. The developer is not responsible for them.
